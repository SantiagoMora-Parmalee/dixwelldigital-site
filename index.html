How it works

You see the finished site before you decide anything.

  1. I build a preview

    I use what's already public on your Google listing: hours, services, photos and your best reviews. It costs you nothing.

  2. You look it over

    I bring it to your shop and show it to you on my phone. Ask for changes, or say no: I delete the preview and you owe nothing.

  3. It goes live and I keep it running

    I handle hosting, your domain and security. When your hours, prices or menu change, text me and it's updated within 3 business days.

Straight answers

What counts as an edit?

Any change to existing text, hours, prices, photos or menu items that takes under 30 minutes. Text or email me and it's done within 3 business days. Unused edits don't roll over. New pages or features are quoted in writing before I start.

Who owns the site?

You own your business name, logo, photos, text and your domain, which is registered in your name. I own the design and code and license them to you while you're a client.

What if I want to stop?

If you're not happy in the first 60 days, tell me and you owe nothing more. After the 3-month minimum, either of us can cancel with 30 days' notice. Within 14 days I send you your content and the code to move your domain anywhere you like.

What if you stop doing this?

Your site stays yours. If I ever stop offering the service, I give you the full site, code included, to keep and use for good, plus your domain and everything you need to move it.

What do I have to do?

Approve the site by text or email before it goes live, then tell me when something changes. That's it.

Why not build it myself on Wix or an AI builder?

Those cost less, but you set the site up and keep it current yourself. With me, you never have to touch it.
