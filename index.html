How it works

You see the finished site before you decide anything.

  1. I build a preview

    I use what's already public on your Google listing: hours, services, photos and your best reviews. It costs you nothing.

  2. You look it over

    I show it to you on a phone at your shop. Ask for changes, or say no and owe nothing.

  3. It goes live and I keep it running

    I handle hosting, your domain and security. When your hours, prices or menu change, text me and I update it.

Every site includes

  • A map and directions to your door
  • Hours that match your Google listing
  • Your best customer reviews, front and center
  • A layout built for phones first
  • Your own .com address, registered in your name
